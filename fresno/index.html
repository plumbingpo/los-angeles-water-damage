Water Damage We See Most in Fresno

Water usually escapes at the same few vulnerable spots in a home. The list below covers what we are asked to clean up most for Fresno owners.

Water heaters: Over the years, mineral deposits settle in a tank and corrode the metal underneath. The first sign is often a rusty puddle or a damp ring on the floor around the base. A split tank does not stop at one tankful, because the inlet keeps feeding it until the valve is shut. Heaters in a hallway closet or attic can ruin flooring and ceilings well beyond the utility area.

Roofs: Wind can lift shingles and drive rain underneath them in a single storm. Skylights, plumbing vents and chimneys are other entry points once their seals crack with age. Since water follows the framing downhill, the ceiling stain rarely sits directly below the actual opening. For roof leaks in Fresno, we tarp where needed, pull out soaked insulation and dry the structure so the ceiling can be repaired.

Freezing: Depending on the season, a hard overnight freeze can burst lines that were fine the day before. The first to go are hose bibs, irrigation lines and any pipe routed through an unheated space. Vacant homes and rooms with the heat turned down are especially exposed. If a faucet only trickles on a freezing morning, shut off the water and call before the pipe thaws.

Backups: A drain that gurgles or several fixtures that slow down together point to a blockage in the main line. Once it closes off fully, anything you flush or drain returns at the lowest opening in the house. The water is unsanitary, and hard surfaces need disinfecting after the solids and liquid are removed. Until a plumber opens the line, do not use sinks or toilets and keep everyone clear of the mess.

Seepage: Dampness at the base of a basement or foundation wall often means water is collecting outside. Short downspouts, soil that slopes toward the house and blocked footing drains are frequent causes. Early clues include powdery white residue on the concrete, flaking wall paint and air that always smells musty. We remove any standing water in Fresno homes, dry the walls and slab, and explain what likely caused it.

How Drying Works on a Fresno Water Damage Job

A surface that feels dry is not proof that a room is dry. The face of drywall often seems normal when the cavity side is damp, and carpet fibers dry long before the padding underneath. For that reason we rely on meters, not on feel. Our Fresno technicians carry probe meters for wood and wallboard, scanning meters that read through finishes, and infrared cameras that pick up damp areas as cooler spots.

Different materials let go of water at different speeds. Carpet and drywall respond quickly, while framing lumber, subfloor, hardwood and concrete hold on much longer. We place air movers and dehumidifiers according to those differences and adjust them as the daily readings change. A span of 3 to 5 days covers most jobs, and we will tell you early if yours looks like an exception.

The expensive error is pulling equipment too soon. Damp materials sealed behind new finishes may mold, buckle or delaminate months afterward. So for every Fresno job we dry to a measured target and keep the readings on file.

Preventing Water Damage in Fresno Homes

A large share of the jobs we attend in Fresno began as a minor fault that could have been caught. Looking over a short list twice a year is enough to find most of those faults.

Begin at the hoses. The rubber hoses on washing machines, dishwashers, toilets and ice makers wear out, and braided stainless replacements are inexpensive. Look under every sink for stains, swollen cabinet floors or a musty smell. Check the water heater for rust at the base and drain a little from the tank once a year to clear sediment. Outside, keep gutters clear and make sure downspouts send water away from the foundation.

Know where your main shutoff valve is and make sure everyone in the house can turn it. Small battery leak alarms near the washing machine, water heater and sinks give you an early warning for very little money. A stain that spreads or a floor that feels soft is worth a call, and Fresno homeowners are welcome to ask us about it.
